For the **Assistant module**, this should be the actual brain/interface of your Personal OS. It is not just a chat screen. It should be able to **understand your request, retrieve the right context, call agents/tools, take actions, explain why it did something, and remember only what it is allowed to remember.**

The core principle:

> **You talk naturally. The Assistant figures out what needs to happen across your entire Personal OS.**

# 65. Final Assistant architecture

```text id="x7m3p9"
                         USER
                           │
                           ▼
                      ASSISTANT
                           │
                    Intent Detection
                           │
                           ▼
                    Context Engine
                           │
                           ▼
                     ORCHESTRATOR
                           │
                    ┌──────┼───────┐
                    ▼      ▼       ▼
                 Planner  Agents  Search
                    │      │       │
                    └──────┼───────┘
                           ▼
                      TOOL ROUTER
                           │
                           ▼
                      POLICY ENGINE
                           │
                  ┌────────┼─────────┐
                  ▼        ▼         ▼
                Tasks    Gmail      Drive
                Finance  Calendar   Files
                           │
                           ▼
                       DATABASE
                           │
                           ▼
                       EVENT BUS
                           │
                           ▼
                       AGENTS
                           │
                           ▼
                    NOTIFICATIONS
```

The key rule is:

> **The Assistant is the interface. The Orchestrator is the brain that coordinates. Agents own specialized work. Tools provide controlled capabilities. The Policy Engine enforces what is allowed. PostgreSQL remains the source of truth.**

That gives you the assistant you're actually describing: you don't need to know whether something lives in Gmail, Drive, Tasks, Finance, Calendar, or Documents. You tell Personal OS **what you want**, and the Assistant figures out **what to find, where to find it, why it needs it, which agent should handle it, what it is allowed to do, and what changed afterward.**
