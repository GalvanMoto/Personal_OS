For the **Documents module**, I would make it different from Files & Assets.

**Files & Assets = the physical digital objects.**
**Documents = the structured knowledge/information extracted from those objects.**

A PDF bank statement is a file. Its structured financial statement, period, account, transactions, issuer, and extracted facts are document data.

A client brief is a file. The **requirements, deadlines, deliverables, decisions, and entities inside it** become document intelligence.

# 1. Document module purpose

Documents should answer:

> What does this document contain, what does it mean, who/what is it related to, what important facts came from it, and what can Personal OS do with that information?

# 2. Document vs File

Keep the relationship like:

```text id="p1r6vo"
FILE
PDF / DOCX / XLSX / JPG
        ↓
DOCUMENT
Semantic representation
        ↓
EXTRACTED INFORMATION
Facts / entities / dates / amounts / requirements
```

Example:

```text
statement_august.pdf
        ↓
Document
"August 2026 SBI Bank Statement"
        ↓
42 transactions
opening balance
closing balance
account
statement period
```

The original file is preserved.

# 3. Document creation

Again, you should have three routes.

### Manual

Create a document record manually.

### Upload

Upload a document and let the system analyze it.

### Automatic

Inbox receives a document → Personal OS automatically creates the document record.

The automatic route should become the main one.

# 50. The distinction I would lock into your architecture

Your **Files & Assets** module answers:

> **"Where is the thing?"**

Your **Documents** module answers:

> **"What does the thing contain and mean?"**

Your **Inbox** answers:

> **"What just arrived and does it matter?"**

Your **Agents** answer:

> **"What should Personal OS do about it?"**

And your **Knowledge/Relationship layer** answers:

> **"What else is this connected to?"**

That separation will make the Personal OS much more powerful and prevent the common mistake of turning the document system into just another file browser.
