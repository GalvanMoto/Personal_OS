For the **Email module**, I would make it an **intelligence layer over your email accounts**, not another Gmail clone.

Its job is to continuously understand incoming/outgoing emails, identify what matters, connect emails to your Personal OS entities, extract tasks/documents/financial information/deadlines, track replies and follow-ups, and give agents controlled access.

Since you already decided that **Inbox is the primary ingestion layer**, Email and Inbox should work together like:

```text
Gmail / Outlook
      ↓
Email Sync
      ↓
Universal Inbox
      ↓
Email Intelligence
      ↓
Tasks / Projects / Clients / Finance / Documents / Calendar
```

# 58. Final Email model

```text id="q3m4j9"
                          EMAIL
                            │
                     Email Account
                            │
                         Threads
                            │
                        Messages
                            │
                       Attachments
                            │
                ┌───────────┼────────────┐
                ↓           ↓            ↓
              TASK       DOCUMENT     FINANCE
                │           │            │
                └───────────┼────────────┘
                            ↓
                         CLIENT
                            │
                         PROJECT
                            │
                           TASK
                            │
                         CONTEXT
                            │
                          AGENTS
```

The most important rule for your implementation is:

> **Email should be a source of information and communication, not the place where every piece of information permanently lives.**

Email tells Personal OS what happened; the appropriate domain owns the resulting data. A client request becomes a Task/Project relationship, a statement becomes a Finance record, a PDF becomes a Document, a meeting proposal becomes Calendar intelligence, and the original email remains attached as the **authoritative source**.

That will keep your Personal OS clean even after thousands of emails are processed.
